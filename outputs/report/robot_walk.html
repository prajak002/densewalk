Six moments along the walk

From m4_final_crowd_walk.mp4: the splat street, the real people re-inserted as cutouts at their tracked ground positions, and the G1 rendered by Isaac from the same camera. Green boxes are the people the robot can perceive: inside its field of view and not hidden behind someone nearer. The inset minimap shows the robot (red) moving down the street. Progress bars show the share of the ~29.8 m start-to-goal distance already covered.

Physics beside visuals

What Isaac simulates vs. what the robot sees

From m4_g1_walk_sidebyside.mp4. Left: the G1 in Isaac Sim on the flat ground-plane collider. That is the entire physics world, because the splat is never a collider. Right: the same pose rendered in the Varanasi splat.

The composite and side-by-side videos come from different runs. At 37.2 s one is holding 8.1 m short of the goal and the other is 2.8 m out. The best run on record, m4_run8.json, finished 0.53 m from the goal after 40 s.

Crowd
Step 4Track every person and vehiclePersistent tracks; each detection's foot point marks where it meets the groundvaranasi_tracks.py

Step 8bCut out people as spritesReal pixels of each person, matched to their trackvaranasi_people_sprites.py · varanasi_match_sprites.py
Robot
ReusedG1 locomotion policyTrained on flat ground, 1500 iterations, mean reward 28.09. Turns a velocity command into walking. Not retrained.g1_policy/model_1499.pt
NavigationChoose the velocity commandRule based on time to collision, with a learned PPO alternative. Sees only people inside its field of view and range who aren't hidden behind nearer people.crowd_nav.py · varanasi_train_nav.py
